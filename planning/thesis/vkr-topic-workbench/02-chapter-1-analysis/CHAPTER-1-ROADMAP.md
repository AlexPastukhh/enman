## Source priority for Chapter 1 drafting

For Chapter 1 topic/full-text chats, use sources in this order:

```text
1. Active topic drafts and confirmed roadmap decisions.
2. Current chapter roadmap and raw notes as cautious source.
3. Scenarios and subject/process rules.
4. Research materials.
5. Visual materials.
6. Existing section drafts.
7. Existing chapter drafts.
8. Legacy chaotic drafts.
```

Existing old drafts / ready chapters / legacy materials are valid candidate material, but they are fallback/cross-check sources. They are used near the end, when current structured sources do not give enough material, when we need to recover a missed idea, or when the user explicitly asks to use a specific draft as an example.
