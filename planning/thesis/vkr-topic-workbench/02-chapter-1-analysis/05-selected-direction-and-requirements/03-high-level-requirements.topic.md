## 1. Зачем эта тема нужна

This topic supports the final Chapter 1 bridge from analysis to design. It turns problem analysis and alternative comparison into the selected development direction, goal, tasks and high-level requirements.

## 2. Куда входит

ПЗ:
- 1.5 Выбор направления разработки, цели, задачи и требования к web-приложению.

Преподаватель должен понять:
- why custom web application is selected;
- what goal follows from the problem;
- which tasks must be solved;
- which high-level requirements guide Chapter 2 design.
